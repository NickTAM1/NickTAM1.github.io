NeoRocket – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

NeoRocket ("the game") is a free, single-player game. Android may show optional Google AdMob advertising: a rewarded video chosen by the player for a coin bonus and occasional paced interstitials after a successful result. There are no banners, and ads are never required to start, retry or continue a flight. Web and iOS builds do not show ads.

Data we collect

The game itself has no account, chat, user posts, location features, analytics or crash-reporting. Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform may process device and advertising information needed to serve, measure and protect optional ads, subject to the consent choices shown by Google. NeoRocket does not sell personal data or add its own tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, upgrades, items and settings) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. Android may expose the advertising ID to Google Mobile Ads when the SDK and consent settings allow it; the game uses conservative child-directed, under-age and G content settings and does not enable personalized treatment from an age choice.

Children

The game does not ask for a birthday or contact details and does not collect children’s information directly. Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process the limited device and advertising information described above when Android ads are enabled, subject to the consent choices shown by Google and the conservative child-directed settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
