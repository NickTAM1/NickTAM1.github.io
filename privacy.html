NeoRocket – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

NeoRocket ("the game") is a free, single-player game. Android may show an optional Google AdMob rewarded ad chosen by the player for one chosen in-game item after a successful new campaign clear. The offer states the exact item before the ad starts. Items can also be earned through play or bought with in-game coins. Automatic interstitial placements are disabled. There are no banners, and ads are never required to start, retry or continue a flight. Web and iOS builds do not show ads.

Data we collect

The game itself has no account, chat, user posts, location features, analytics or crash-reporting. Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions such as views and taps, app and SDK diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to SDK configuration and available privacy choices. The Android build requests restricted data processing from Google and applies child-directed, under-age-of-consent and G-rated ad treatment to all users. These settings restrict advertising; they do not mean that the SDK collects no data. Google states that Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit using TLS. NeoRocket does not add its own tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, upgrades, items, flight contracts and settings) is saved locally. NeoRocket does not operate a cloud-save service. Android system backup or device transfer may retain or restore app data according to your device settings. Uninstalling removes the local copy; reinstalling without a system restore starts fresh.

Permissions

The game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. The Android build removes the advertising-ID permission, preventing access through that permission; Google may still process other SDK identifiers. The game does not enable personalized treatment from an age choice.

Your choices

Ads are not required for play. In Settings, Privacy Options opens Google's available consent controls on supported Android builds. Privacy Policy opens this page. You can reset game progress in Settings or remove local app data through Android settings. Advertising ID controls are available in Android settings. Google's privacy policy explains its own data handling.

Children

The game does not ask for a birthday or contact details and does not collect children’s information directly. Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process the limited device and advertising information described above when Android ads are enabled, subject to available privacy choices and conservative child-directed settings. The under-age setting suppresses Google's European consent prompt; no age choice in the game enables adult ad treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
